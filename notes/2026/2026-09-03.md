### Forking unmaintained plugins for v10

**TSC Summary:** Two popular plugins, `eslint-plugin-jsx-a11y` ([jsx-eslint/eslint-plugin-jsx-a11y#1075](https://github.com/jsx-eslint/eslint-plugin-jsx-a11y/issues/1075)) and `eslint-plugin-react` ([jsx-eslint/eslint-plugin-react#3956](https://github.com/jsx-eslint/eslint-plugin-react/issues/3956)), have refused to update for v10, causing confusion and frustration. The TSC discussed whether to fork these into `eslint-community`.

* @mdjermanovic noted it's already seven months past the first v10.x release, which seems like too long to wait, and asked about existing alternatives.
* @mdjermanovic pointed to [`@eslint-react/eslint-plugin`](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@eslint-react/eslint-plugin) as an alternative to `eslint-plugin-react`.
* For `jsx-a11y`, @mdjermanovic found [`eslint-plugin-jsx-a11y-x`](https://www.npmjs.com/package/eslint-plugin-jsx-a11y-x) mentioned on the issue.
* @fasttime noted it only has 64k downloads per week.
* @nzakas noted it's only four months old, its README hasn't been updated for `defineConfig()`, it has only some of the original rules, and hasn't been touched since its last release, but per its `package.json` it does support ESLint v10 (per @mdjermanovic).
* @nzakas suggested asking the `eslint-plugin-jsx-a11y-x` maintainers if they'd like to donate it to `eslint-community` rather than starting fresh, and @mdjermanovic and @fasttime agreed.

**Resolution:** The TSC agreed to recommend `@eslint-react/eslint-plugin` as a replacement for `eslint-plugin-react`, and to reach out to the maintainers of `eslint-plugin-jsx-a11y-x` to see if they'd like to donate it to `eslint-community`. The community team will be asked to make contact.

### [`array-callback-return` false negative with `Object.groupBy()`/`Map.groupBy()`](https://github.com/eslint/eslint/issues/21113)

**TSC Summary:** A contributor added this to the agenda, raising a case where a missing `return` inside `Object.groupBy()`/`Map.groupBy()` callbacks isn't caught, and asking whether this pattern belongs in `array-callback-return` or a new rule.

* @mdjermanovic noted `Object.groupBy()` and `Map.groupBy()` are ES2024 features worth supporting, but since they aren't array methods (though they take an array or generic iterable as their first argument, per @fasttime and @nzakas), they don't obviously belong in `array-callback-return`.
* @nzakas asked whether to create a rule narrowly targeting these two methods, or something broader with room to grow; @mdjermanovic and @fasttime both favored a broader, generic rule that could bundle in future similar methods.
* @fasttime asked if such a rule would also cover callbacks of other built-ins, like `Promise` methods.
* @mdjermanovic noted ESLint core previously had a rule named `callback-return` (see [archived docs](https://archive.eslint.org/docs/rules/callback-return)), so reusing that exact name could be confusing, and suggested continuing the naming/scope discussion on the issue itself.

**Resolution:** The TSC agreed that `Object.groupBy` and `Map.groupBy` do not belong in `array-callback-return`, and that a new, generic rule should be created to cover both array callbacks and these new methods. Further discussion on the new rule's name and exact scope will continue on the issue.
